Verification

A certificate is not the only proof.

Many of the best kalari and silambam masters learned from a guru, not a course. So we check the line of teaching and show it on the profile.

GuruNamed, with school and place
TeacherYears trained, references, video review
YouSee the whole chain before you book
  1. Everyone

    Identity

    Government ID matched to a live selfie, phone number confirmed by OTP. Documents stay private and never show on the profile.

  2. Where a certificate exists

    Credentials

    Yoga teacher training, fitness certifications and medical or Ayurveda degrees are checked with the issuing body where possible. The issuer is named on the profile.

  3. Kalari, silambam

    Lineage

    Named guru and school, two references from other practitioners, a recorded demonstration, and a visit from our team in launch cities.

  4. After joining

    Trial-tested

    The badge appears once a teacher keeps a 4.5 rating across their first ten sessions, and can be removed if it falls.
